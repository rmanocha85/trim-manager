# Patient workspace update — October 30, 2026

The roster has a name/PHN/room search and shows “New patient” during the first 14 calendar days after a genuinely new panel addition. This is ordinary text for the browser's Find command. Returning retained patients keep their existing identity and do not become new admissions.

Roster, Billing and Notes show the patient's Last, First name and copy control, with aligned PHN/copy, age and designation columns. The name itself opens the Ava chart in a new tab. Age is calculated from a recorded date of birth. MOST is projected from the Markdown in the individual PHN patient records. The latest identifiable documented MOST is shown using the six exact form labels. Proposed changes remain in the hover evidence and never replace a recorded designation. Conflicting undated designations remain unset with a pending-review explanation. Missing information stays blank. DOB can be recorded on the roster; a reported age is never used to invent a DOB.

The local service maintains `patient-display.json` as a disposable display projection in the existing Routine notes folder. It contains no independent clinical authority and can be rebuilt from the individual patient files. The cloud app reads the synced projection only when its dataset matches. Clinical data stays outside this repository. On the laptop, profile reads and uploads use the existing local service even when billing is connected through Google.

The usual billing connection requests only `drive.file`, which cannot access pre-existing clinical folders unless already granted. Cloud users can explicitly choose “Connect patient records.” The consent dialog discloses that Google's Drive permission covers all Drive files, and offers the laptop alternative. Only its Continue button requests that broader scope; ordinary billing sign-in never does. A separate, in-memory connection must match the billing account and pass private-folder checks. No token is persisted or sent to the laptop. The connection searches for an existing folder and refuses inaccessible or ambiguous targets rather than creating a duplicate workspace.

Documents opens an upload dialog from the roster or patient box. It accepts PDF, PNG, JPG and WebP, including dropped files and pasted screenshots, up to six files and 10 MB total. It uploads originals to the existing private Routine notes/Incoming folder, verifies their SHA-256 hashes, then writes an intake manifest. Retries reuse the same filenames/Drive IDs. Sources are retained.

The local service scans only explicit app intake manifests, never arbitrary files in Incoming. It processes queued jobs through the existing ChatGPT connection when available. Cloud-only uploads remain queued until Drive sync and the local service are available. No AI credentials are shipped to GitHub Pages. AI failures are visible and retryable; a missing or mismatched source PHN blocks importing. Every attached document must identify the selected PHN.

Matched source documents are interpreted to the best of the model's ability and automatically saved for clinical review, with uncertain wording qualified in the narrative. Content-completeness concerns do not trigger an AI audit veto, hold, or new warning. Relevant medication discontinuations are retained as dated treatment history, not new orders. Exact source PHN/roster matching, source integrity and conditional save/read-back remain mandatory. Concurrent target edits automatically retry against the newer profile. This never creates a visit, order, signature or billing; originals are retained.

The Local changes dialog has less status text and closes after all session changes save successfully. Conflicts, failures and unfinished patient forms keep it open. Existing discard protections remain.

Validation uses synthetic records and a fake AI response. Browser tests cover upload and read-back locally and through a mocked Drive API, including interrupted upload retries. A live synthetic PDF plus screenshot also passed through the existing ChatGPT connection (gpt-6.1-sol), with both source PHNs extracted correctly. No real patient documents were sent as a deployment smoke test.


October 31 compact layout: the approved 200px name content column puts PHNs next to names in a fixed, aligned column. Hospital status follows PHN; age and MOST remain on the right, with headings on the same grid. Both rosters share compact rows; long names wrap. Numeric Ava IDs are hidden from rows and retained for chart links and editing. Billing cards select billing when their background is clicked; the name and copy controls act independently.

Patient counts and Add patient sit beside the roster tabs. Add patient targets the active My Patients or Other Patients collection, with doctor assignment for Other Patients and cross-collection identity validation. Reload and print actions sit in More; the shared Save to Drive control remains. Row Documents/Edit/Remove actions sit in an overflow menu. Other Patients retains search and doctor filtering without duplicate headings or statistic cards.

The display vocabulary follows [MOST updated 2023](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1ZWz9S8zmkv-VIImBuZziQ4l2KnuEciUI/view), Fraser Health ADDI105016D, April 17, 2023: CPR C2, DNACPR C2, DNACPR C1, DNACPR M3, DNACPR M2, DNACPR M1. Bare C2 is ambiguous; bare M1/M2/M3/C1 map to the form's DNACPR labels. This display normalization does not change clinical orders or source records. Validated private physician confirmations take precedence; original history is retained on hover.

November 2 billing selection: editing or clearing the patient search immediately deselects the patient and removes the billing controls. The panel asks the user to select a result, even for a single match. Existing pending or saved billing stays unchanged. This also clears the editing ID of an entry opened from History, and applies to Concern and Routine Billing.

November 2 roster removal: My Patients and Other Patients both offer “Remove from roster” in each row’s actions menu. Confirmation names the patient and source roster. Removal retains the full record (including doctor assignment) in Removed Patients for the existing three calendar months, preserves saved/pending billing, and uses the existing local recovery and Save to Drive flow. Concurrent edits still require reconciliation.

November 3 location follow-up completed November 5: Other Patients now offers “Edit unit & room” in each row menu. Choose an existing shared unit or “Unit not recorded”; room may remain blank or be entered independently. The unknown-unit section remains available for gradual completion. Editing preserves physician assignment, identity, flags and billing. Unfinished location forms recover after reload; Save stages locally, then Save to Drive commits.

November 5 hospital return: both live rosters show “Returned today” beside an open hospital admission. One click records the current Pacific date locally without confirmation, independent of the Billing calendar. The existing Save to Drive action commits it. Undo restores the exact previous stay while preserving unrelated roster changes, survives local recovery, and is removed once the return is saved or superseded. The row menu opens the shared hospital date editor for other dates. Existing form, history, conflict and save locks apply; patient data and billing are not changed by release testing.

November 5 patient flags: existing `pFlag` continues to mean psychiatric consult, now displayed as a blue Ψ. Shared patient badges show both icons in Roster, Notes and Billing; full meanings are available as tooltips and accessible labels. Each active roster's row menu can toggle either flag, and Add/My Patients Edit include both. Changes use existing encrypted local recovery, conflict reconciliation and explicit Save to Drive. Palliative patients appear once in a top section of their own roster, with unit and room shown; search and doctor filters apply. Flags never infer diagnoses, change roster ownership or create billing.

November 5 Focused connection design: the welcome and Google connection cards use the selected narrow layout, TRIM branding and a single prominent connection action. The main Google prompt omits the repeated email/account row. Remembered account, account switching, file override and laptop-folder fallback remain under collapsed Connection options. Error/retry, first-time setup, cancel, keyboard focus and mobile layouts remain supported; laptop automatic opening and existing OAuth permissions are unchanged.


November 7 workflow refinements: new patients in either roster show a Med reconciliation done checkbox during the same first 14 calendar days as the New patient badge (days 0 through 13, Pacific date). Checking records the completion date through local recovery and explicit Save to Drive; unchecking clears it. The checkbox hides on day 14 while the recorded date remains in the roster. Historical lists and locked forms remain read-only.

Conference codes 13121 and 14077 now use plain editable 24-hour Start time and End time fields; Tab moves directly between them. Their existing default remains the current Pacific time rounded to a 10-minute boundary with a 10-minute duration. Same-patient, same-day 00114 diagnoses, including multiple diagnoses and an already-saved 114, populate linked conference items. Explicit conference diagnosis edits remain independent.

Save status occupies the existing fixed header slot, with pending, saving, verified Drive save, local-folder save, and persistent failure/retry states. Routine saves no longer insert a yellow bar or move the roster. Clicking the status opens details. Local-folder saves explicitly do not claim Google Drive sync verification. Document-connection actions remain in the footer.

Validation uses synthetic records, including day-13/day-14 boundaries, reload recovery, both roster collections, manual time/diagnosis overrides, partial-save failures and retries, conflict resolution, and desktop/mobile layout stability. No real patient records are changed by release tests.
